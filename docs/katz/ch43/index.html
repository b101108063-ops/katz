As described earlier, RANKL is produced by osteoblasts. It stimulates osteoclastogenesis via RANK, the receptor for RANKL that is present on osteoclasts and osteoclast precursors. By interfering with RANKL function, denosumab inhibits osteoclast formation and activity. It is at least as effective as the potent bisphosphonates in inhibiting bone resorption and has recently been approved for treatment of post- menopausal osteoporosis and some cancers (prostate and breast). The latter application is to limit the development of bone metas- tases and bone loss resulting from the use of drugs suppressing gonadal function. denosumab is administered subcutaneously every 6 months, which avoids gastrointestinal side effects. The drug appears to be well tolerated but two concerns remain. First, a number of cells in the immune system also express RANKL, suggesting that there could be an increased risk of infection associ- ated with the use of denosumab. Second, because the suppression of bone turnover with denosumab is similar to that of the potent bisphosphonates, the risk of osteonecrosis of the jaw and subtro- chanteric fractures may be increased, although this has not been reported in the clinical trials leading to its approval.

重點摘要

  • PTH、維生素D和FGF23構成骨質礦物質恆定的核心調節系統，三者對腸道、骨頭和腎臟有不同的作用
  • 抑鈣素能抑制骨吸收、降低血鈣和血磷，用於治療Paget’s disease、高鈣血症和骨質疏鬆症
  • 雙膦酸鹽類藥物（如alendronate、risedronate、zoledronate）能抑制骨吸收，是治療骨質疏鬆症的主要藥物
  • Denosumab是抗RANKL單株抗體，能有效抑制蝕骨細胞生成和活性
  • 雌激素和選擇性雌激素受體調節劑（SERM）可用於預防和治療停經後骨質疏鬆症
